# Fusion Training Status — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 18, 2026

Fusion Training Status is an independent Garmin Connect IQ watch application with a companion application for Android. It provides informational training-load and recovery estimates. This policy describes the current private beta (Beta V2).

## Information accessed

With your device permissions and the relevant platform services, the app may access:

- Garmin workout history summaries, including activity identifiers, start times and durations, for workout frequency, volume and load calculations.
- Health Connect sleep session information, heart-rate variability (HRV/RMSSD) and resting heart rate records on the paired Android phone.
- Basic device and synchronization state used for pairing, data freshness and troubleshooting.

The app does not request precise location or ANT+ profile access. Unavailable health metrics are shown as missing; no fabricated measurements are substituted.

## How information is used and stored

The Android companion processes authorized health observations on your device, combines them with workout summaries and sends a limited, versioned training-status result to your paired Garmin watch using the Garmin Connect IQ platform.

The Android companion does not persist raw Health Connect observations. The watch may cache the most recent validated, derived training-status result in Garmin application storage so the last status can remain visible when the phone is disconnected.

The current implementation has **no Fusion-operated cloud service**, no developer-controlled transfer of workout or health records, no advertising network and no analytics SDK. Data exchanged through Garmin Connect IQ and stored by Health Connect remains subject to the relevant platform providers' separate terms and privacy policies. This description does not guarantee that Garmin, Android or Health Connect themselves have no data processing.

## Sharing and sale

Fusion does not sell personal training or health data, use it for advertising or intentionally transfer it to a Fusion-operated external server. The companion/watch transport uses the official Garmin mechanisms on your paired devices.

## Your choices and deletion

Health Connect read permissions are optional and may be revoked in Android settings or Health Connect. To remove locally cached app results, clear the relevant app's data or uninstall Fusion Training Status from the watch; uninstalling the Android companion clears data belonging to that application. Removing Fusion does not remove independent training records stored in Garmin Connect, Health Connect or their source applications.

## Security

The applications use limited permissions, bounded and validated messages and app-level storage controls. They depend on Garmin's official paired-device messaging mechanisms and Android's Health Connect permission model. No system can guarantee perfect security.

## Health disclaimer

Training and recovery estimates are informational and do not diagnose, treat, prevent or monitor medical conditions.

## Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated before any change that materially alters the categories of data accessed or the way it is used, stored or disclosed. The published policy link should remain available if the application is updated.

## Contact

For privacy requests or questions, use the developer contact email shown in the Fusion Training Status listing in the Garmin Connect IQ Store. The developer should provide an actively monitored contact address there.
